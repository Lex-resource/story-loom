## Controlled change

A10 keeps A8/A9's independent layered-memory path, atomic publication,
deterministic handoff, chapter contract, character cards, conditional evidence
surface rules, and no extra Polisher. V25 adds only deterministic and shared
rules:

- an empty `item_state_ledger` explicitly means no new paper, record page,
  original, copy, attachment, tool, holder, or transfer may be asserted;
- evidence may be recorded in the interface, by spoken recitation, by pure
  observation, or on an already-present device;
- a closed read-only window may only be expanded or viewed as an existing
  monitor list, never reopened or treated as a new device response;
- Editor can repair wording locally, while Validator blocks only actual
  violation of these explicit boundaries.

The new `item_state_rules` are passed through the same contract rendered for
Planner, Writer, Editor, Validator, and Extractor. No dedicated model call is
added. A10 uses an independent project, the fixed chapter-1 seed, hybrid
recall disabled, and one auto batch for generated chapters 2-10.
